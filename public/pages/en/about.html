About HPS Power Solutions

We are the technology partner for demanding energy and automation projects.

Our mission

Innovation, sustainability, and reliability are at the heart of what we do. HPS Power Solutions unites electrical engineering, software development, and deep engineering know-how under one roof. We build solutions that reduce energy consumption, automate processes, and enable new business models.

With an interdisciplinary team of engineers, software developers, and service specialists we partner with our customers over the long term.
