Power electronics

Our power modules cover voltages from 400 V to 24 kV and enable flexible configurations for converters, energy recovery, and microgrids. Compact designs, water-cooled systems, and redundant safety mechanisms ensure maximum availability.

  • Scalable cabinet modules with pluggable inserts
  • Silicon-carbide power stages for highest efficiency
  • Integrated safety technology compliant with IEC 61508

System integration

We connect automation, energy storage, charging infrastructure, and process control systems into a centralized platform. Open interfaces (OPC UA, Modbus, MQTT) enable seamless integration with existing IT landscapes.
