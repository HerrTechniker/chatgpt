How the tour works

Use your Insta360 X4 to export an equirectangular 360° image (JPG or PNG) or embed a tour published via Insta360 Studio. The viewer below loads a placeholder by default; swap in your own assets to guide visitors through your facilities.

  • Supports 360° photos (JPG/PNG) rendered from Insta360 Studio or the Insta360 mobile app.
  • Optional deep link to a hosted Insta360 tour or an external 360° video.
  • Step-by-step instructions below explain how to refresh the experience.

The placeholder panorama is shown. Follow the steps below to publish your Insta360 capture.

Publish your own Insta360 X4 footage

  1. Export a 360° photo: Open your recording in Insta360 Studio and export an equirectangular JPG (2:1 aspect ratio, e.g. 7680×3840 px).
  2. Store the file: Place the file inside public/tour/ within this project and name it, for example, factory-tour.jpg.
  3. Update the reference: Adjust the data-panorama attribute in this section (or via your deployment tooling) to point to the new file, e.g. /tour/factory-tour.jpg.
  4. Optional: Embed a hosted tour. When using an Insta360-hosted tour, replace the viewer with an iFrame link instead; consult the README’s “Virtual tour” section for details.

Combine multiple perspectives by adding additional viewer containers or by defining hotspots directly inside the viewer configuration. The current integration is powered by Photo Sphere Viewer and supports fullscreen mode on desktop and gesture controls on mobile devices.
