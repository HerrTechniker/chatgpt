Internship FAQ

Which disciplines are we looking for?

We welcome applications from electrical engineering, computer science, mechatronics, industrial engineering, and related fields.

How long does an internship last?

Mandatory internships usually run 3–6 months. We define voluntary internships and working student positions individually based on your study progress and availability.

Is the internship paid?

Yes, all internships and working student roles are paid fairly. We discuss the details during the personal interview.
